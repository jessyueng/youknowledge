## Credit Care

Recover credits when AI results don't meet expectations.

### How It Works

1. Review your version history
2. Select the version to rewind to
3. Credit Care restores all credits used since that version
4. Your project reverts to the selected state

### Monthly Allowances

| Plan | Credit Care Uses |
|------|------------------|
| Free | 1/month |
| Pro | 30/month |
| Starter ($40) | 60/month |
| Premium ($100) | 120/month |
| Ultra/Business ($200) | 150/month |
| Enterprise ($500) | 200/month |

### Rules

- Zero-cost commits (manual edits) don't count toward allowance
- Allowances reset monthly (don't accumulate)
- Pre-confirmation shows exact refund amount
- Full transparency in transaction history

## Plan Details

### Free Plan ($0/month)

**Best For:**
- Learning YouWare basics
- Exploring platform capabilities
- Personal hobby projects

**Includes:**
- Trial credits to get started
- 5 projects maximum
- 1 Credit Care use per month
- Basic features only

**Does NOT Include:**
- YouBase backend
- Custom domains
- Code download
- Credit rollovers

---

### Pro Plan ($20/month)

**Best For:**
- Freelance designers and developers
- Building and launching real products
- Regular AI-assisted development

**Includes:**
- 2,000 + 1,000 bonus credits monthly (3,000 total)
- Up to 100 projects
- 30 Credit Care uses per month
- YouBase (database, auth, storage, secrets)
- Custom domain support
- Download and edit code
- Hide from community
- Remove YouWare badge
- Credit rollovers month-to-month

---

### Ultra Plan ($200/month)

**Best For:**
- Professional developers and agencies
- Scaling and automating business
- Enterprise-level requirements

**Includes:**
- 20,000 + 16,000 bonus credits monthly (36,000 total)
- Unlimited projects
- 150 Credit Care uses per month
- All Pro features plus:
  - Private projects
  - Database restore capability
  - One-on-one dedicated service
  - Early access to new features
  - Opt out of data training

## Subscription Management

### Upgrading

- Takes effect immediately
- Receive credit difference right away
- Access new features instantly

### Downgrading

- Takes effect at next billing cycle
- Keep access until period ends
- Existing credits remain in account

### Credit Validity

- Valid for **2 years** after purchase
- Credits remain valid even after downgrading
- No expiration during active subscription

### Cancellation

- Cancel anytime with no penalties
- Keep access until end of billing period
- Credits remain valid for 2 years
- Can reactivate anytime

### Billing Management

Access in **Account Settings → Billing**:
- View and download invoices
- Update billing information
- Track credit usage in Wallet/Creator Center
